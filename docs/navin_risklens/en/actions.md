# RiskLens actions - 9 cards

Each card sends `/risklens` with a precise specification; your brief is appended. No action starts implementing the project.

## Run

| Action | Delivers |
| --- | --- |
| Full RiskLens | Full session: minimum context, 6-month failure frame, failure reasons, parallel deep-dives, synthesis (most likely / most dangerous failure, hidden assumption, revised plan, 3-5 item checklist), `risklens-report-*.html` and `risklens-transcript-*.md` files. |
| Product / feature launch | Product-oriented risklens before any code: audience mismatch, adoption friction, scope creep, competitive timing, reachable success metric. Report + revised plan + checklist. |
| Go-to-market launch | Launch risklens where money / reputation is on the line: pricing approval friction, channel reach, message-market fit, prep time vs opportunity cost, compounding post-launch risks. |

## Decisions

| Action | Delivers |
| --- | --- |
| Hire or role | Why this hire fails before the offer: role ambiguity, wrong seniority, culture fit, ramp time, org-design problem disguised as a hire. Revised role brief + pre-offer checklist. |
| Pricing / business model | What breaks if the pricing or model shift fails: willingness to pay, approval chains, cannibalization, unit economics, switching costs. Test plan (pilot price / cohort) + go/no-go checklist. |
| Partnership or deal | Why the partnership dies after the handshake: misaligned incentives, unclear ownership, dependency risk, reputation spillover, exit terms. Revised deal guardrails + pre-signature checklist. |
| Strategy pivot | Blind spots in a positioning or strategy shift: capability gaps, brand confusion, channel access, competitor response, measurable success criteria. Revised strategy + early warning signs + pre-commit checklist. |

## Focus

| Action | Delivers |
| --- | --- |
| Hidden assumptions | RiskLens centered on the single biggest unquestioned assumption, then early warning signs and the smallest experiment that would falsify it this week. Report files included. |
| Pre-launch checklist only | RiskLens focused on 3-5 verifiable actions that prevent or detect the top failure modes, plus a short synthesis (most likely failure + hidden assumption). |
