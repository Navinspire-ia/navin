# Templates — 25 models, 5 themes

Each card sends `/studio` with a precise specification. Your optional brief (top field) is appended to every one.

## Project

| Template | Format | Generates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project pitch deck | PPTX | 10-12 slides: vision, problem, solution, approach, timeline, team, risks, next steps. Strong visual hierarchy, one idea per slide. |
| Project charter | DOCX | Context, SMART objectives, scope in/out, stakeholders and roles, governance, milestones, budget summary, risks and mitigations. |
| Roadmap & planning | XLSX | Phases, workstreams, tasks with owners, start/end dates, dependencies, milestone sheet, status dashboard with conditional formatting. |
| Status report | PDF | One page: overall RAG status, progress vs plan, key metrics, accomplishments, blockers, decisions needed, next period plan. |
| Project budget | XLSX | Cost breakdown by category, forecast vs actual with variance, monthly burn rate chart data, summary sheet. |

## Investment

| Template | Format | Generates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investor pitch deck | PPTX | 12-15 slides: problem, solution, product, TAM/SAM/SOM, business model, traction, competition, team, projections, funding ask and use of funds. |
| Business plan | DOCX | Executive summary, company, market analysis, competitive landscape, go-to-market, operations, team, 3-year financial plan, risks. |
| 3-year financial model | XLSX | Assumptions sheet, revenue build-up, cost structure, P&L, cash flow, headcount plan, unit economics (CAC, LTV), scenario toggles. |
| Investor one-pager | PDF | Elevator pitch, problem/solution, traction metrics, market, business model, team highlights, funding ask — dense but scannable. |
| Due diligence pack | DOCX | Data-room checklist (legal, financial, tech, HR), company memorandum structure, investor Q&A preparation. |

## Marketing

| Template | Format | Generates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing plan | PPTX | Objectives and KPIs, target segments, positioning, channel strategy, campaign calendar, budget allocation, measurement plan. |
| Campaign brief | DOCX | Background, objectives and KPIs, audience and insight, key message and proof points, deliverables, channels, timeline, budget. |
