# Entity & Company Research

## Overview

Build a sourced company/person dossier. Separate facts from inference.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the entity (legal name, domain, geography).
2. Gather from official site, registries, LinkedIn-like public pages, news, job posts (tech stack signals).
3. Structure the dossier:

```markdown
## Entity
- name / domain / HQ

## What they do
...

## People
- ...

## Signals
- revenue / funding / headcount (cite)
- tech (from jobs, engineering blog)

## Contacts / next steps
- ...

## Sources
- ...
```

4. Mark confidence (high/med/low) per claim.
5. Never invent emails or phone numbers — only cite found contacts.

## Rules

- Comply with user policies on personal data.
- Prefer primary sources; label blogs as secondary.
