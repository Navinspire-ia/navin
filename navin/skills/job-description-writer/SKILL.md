# Job Description Writer

## Overview

A job description is an ad AND a filter. Attract the right candidates, repel the wrong ones, and enable fair screening.

## Structure

1. **Title** — searchable, standard ("Développeur Backend Python" not "Code Ninja")
2. **Company pitch** — 3 lines: what you do, why it matters, team context
3. **The mission** — what this person will own and what success looks like at 6/12 months
4. **Responsibilities** — 5–8 bullets, verbs first, ordered by time actually spent
5. **Requirements** — split MUST (3–5 max) vs NICE; every must-have is truly eliminatory
6. **What we offer** — salary range (state it), remote policy, growth, real perks
7. **Process** — steps and timeline (respect reduces drop-off)

## Craft rules

- Cut requirement inflation: 10 must-haves = qualified people don't apply
- Gender-neutral language; FR: "(H/F)" and neutral formulations
- "Success at 6 months" beats a vague responsibility list — candidates self-select
- Salary transparency improves both volume and quality of applicants

## Workflow

1. Interview the hiring manager: why this role now, top 3 outcomes, deal-breakers, budget.
2. Draft; test each requirement — "would we reject a great candidate lacking this?"
3. `proofreader` pass; produce versions: full (site), short (LinkedIn post via `social-media-manager`), outreach blurb.
4. Feed the criteria grid to `candidate-screening` so screening matches the ad.

## Rules

- Never post a range you won't honor.
- Legal compliance: no discriminatory criteria (age, situation familiale, etc.).
