# Newsletter example

Format: 20 to 25 bullets in 3 to 5 sections, one or two sentences per bullet,
"we" voice, a link in most bullets (shown here as `[link]`). Written for a
company of several hundred people; team-level detail stays in status updates.
All content below is fictional.

```
Navinspire monthly - March

Announcements
- We opened the Lyon office on 4 March; 18 people are based there, mostly
  engineering and support. Floor plan and badge process: [link]
- Studio 2.4 is live for every customer. The highlights are the Montage video
  editor, the shared task board and a faster file preview. Release notes: [link]
- Our new VP Customer Success, Lina Haddad, starts on 7 April. She joins from
  a Paris SaaS scale-up and will own support, onboarding and renewals.
- The Q2 priorities memo is published: marketplace launch, SOC 2, and a 30
  percent reduction in time-to-first-value for new workspaces. Memo: [link]

Progress on priorities
- Marketplace: 27 partner plugins passed validation, beta opens 24 March for
  40 invited customers. Partner guide: [link]
- Security: SOC 2 Type 1 evidence is 80 percent collected; the auditor
  fieldwork is booked for the week of 14 April.
- Onboarding: the new guided setup cut median time-to-first-desk from 41 to 23
  minutes for workspaces created after 10 March. Dashboard: [link]
- Reliability: 99.96 percent availability in February, one incident (see the
  review: [link]), no customer data affected.

Customers and market
- We signed 3 enterprise accounts this month, including our first in the
  Gulf region. Case study draft for review: [link]
- The Tenders desk now pulls notices from 6 official sources; customers
  submitted 212 bids through it in Q1 so far.
- Two conference talks are confirmed for May (Paris and Dubai); slides and
  speaker notes are in the shared folder: [link]

People
- Welcome to our 4 new colleagues: two engineers on the platform squad, one
  account executive, one support specialist. Intro posts: [link]
- Internal mobility: the design team has an opening for a product designer;
  internal candidates apply before 31 March. Posting: [link]
- Reminder: the engagement survey closes Friday. 61 percent have answered;
  last year we reached 84 percent.

Around the company
- The Friday demo recordings are now in one playlist: [link]
- The running club meets Tuesday at 12:30 from the Paris lobby; all levels.
- Photos from the Lyon opening: [link]
```

Notes for the writer:

- Sections can be renamed to fit the month (for example "Launches",
  "Hiring", "In the press"). Keep three to five of them.
- Each bullet answers "what changed for the reader" or "where do I read more".
- Do not repeat something already announced company-wide unless there is a
  new fact.
- When the source material is thin, say so to the user rather than padding
  with generic lines.
