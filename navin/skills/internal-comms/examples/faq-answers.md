# FAQ answers example

Format: one question in one sentence, one answer in one or two sentences, a
link to the authoritative source (shown as `[link]`). Mark uncertainty and
flag questions that need an official answer. All content is fictional.

```
Questions we heard this week

Q: Does the Lyon office change where I can work from?
A: No. The remote policy is unchanged (up to 3 days a week from home for
hybrid roles); Lyon is an additional office you can book a desk in. Policy:
[link]

Q: When will Studio 2.4 be enabled for customers on the self-hosted plan?
A: Self-hosted packages ship on 2 April after the installer tests finish.
Release plan: [link]

Q: Are the Q2 priorities replacing the team OKRs?
A: No. The three company priorities frame the quarter; teams write their own
OKRs under them by 29 March. Template: [link]

Q: Why did the February incident not trigger a status-page update?
A: The impact was limited to background jobs, which the status page did not
cover at the time. The incident review added a "background processing"
component to the page. Review: [link]

Q: Will the marketplace beta affect my customers' existing plugins?
A: No change for installed plugins. Only invited customers see the marketplace
tab during the beta. FAQ for account teams: [link]

Q: Is there a budget for conference travel this year?
A: Not confirmed. Finance is finalising the travel envelope and has promised
an answer at the April all-hands. (Needs an official reply from Finance.)

Q: How are the new support hires split between regions?
A: One is in Paris and one is in Lyon; both cover the European time zone.
Gulf coverage is being discussed with the new VP Customer Success starting 7
April. (Partial answer; owner: Customer Success.)
```

Notes for the writer:

- Gather questions from where people ask them: team channels, all-hands
  threads, support escalations, the user's notes. Prefer questions that affect
  many people over niche ones.
- Base answers on official communications. When none exists, write the best
  known answer and label it "(Needs an official reply from ...)".
- Keep the tone professional and approachable; no jokes about the question.
- Group related questions when the batch is long (Policy, Product, People).
