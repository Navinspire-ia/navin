# Moss

Mood: Forest floor after rain; soft greens, dark bark, quiet confidence.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#f3f5ee` - pale lichen ground
- **Surface**: `#e4eadb` - cards and callouts
- **Text**: `#1f2a1c` - dark bark for type
- **Accent**: `#6fbf6a` - growth, success, emphasis (Leaf)
- **Secondary**: `#c6e88a` - highlights and a second data series (Lime)

## Typography

- **Heading**: Lora
- **Body**: Inter

## Mode

Light by default. Dark variant: Background `#1f2a1c`, Surface `#283626`, Text `#f3f5ee`; Accent becomes Lime, Secondary becomes Leaf.

## Best for

Sustainability reports, outdoor and wellbeing brands, botanical content, education.
