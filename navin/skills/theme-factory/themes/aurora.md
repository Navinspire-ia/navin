# Aurora

Mood: Night sky lit from below; violet, sky and a thread of lime over near-black. Dramatic but disciplined.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#121214` - deep ground (Graphite)
- **Surface**: `#1c1c24` - panels with a faint violet cast
- **Text**: `#f4f3ee` - headings and body (Paper)
- **Accent**: `#7c3aed` - emphasis and section markers (Violet)
- **Secondary**: `#0ea5e9` - links, charts, a cooler second voice (Sky)

## Typography

- **Heading**: Space Grotesk
- **Body**: Inter

## Mode

Dark by default. Light variant: Background `#f4f3ee`, Surface `#ece9f6`, Text `#17171a`; Accent stays Violet, Secondary becomes `#0e7490`.

## Best for

Launch keynotes, AI and data products, event branding, immersive landing pages.
