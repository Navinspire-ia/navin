# Terracotta

Mood: Baked clay, warm plaster and a cool shadow; generous and grounded.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#faf3ea` - warm plaster ground
- **Surface**: `#f1e2d0` - sand cards and sidebars
- **Text**: `#2b1d16` - burnt umber for all type
- **Accent**: `#ff5f57` - emphasis and the single call to action (Coral)
- **Secondary**: `#0e7490` - cool counterpoint for links and a second series (Teal)

## Typography

- **Heading**: Fraunces
- **Body**: Inter

## Mode

Light by default. Dark variant: Background `#2b1d16`, Surface `#3a2820`, Text `#faf3ea`; Accent stays Coral, Secondary becomes `#f1c38e`.

## Best for

Hospitality, food and craft brands, culture programmes, warm storytelling decks.
