# Slate

Mood: Pure grayscale with a single violet pulse; clean, contemporary, architectural.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#faf9f6` - near-white ground (Cream)
- **Surface**: `#ecebe7` - cards and dividers
- **Text**: `#17171a` - headings and body (Ink)
- **Accent**: `#7c3aed` - one emphasis per view (Violet)
- **Secondary**: `#5f5f63` - muted labels and captions

## Typography

- **Heading**: Manrope
- **Body**: Inter

## Mode

Light by default. Dark variant: Background `#121214`, Surface `#1d1d21`, Text `#f4f3ee`; Accent becomes `#a78bfa`, Secondary becomes `#9a9a96`.

## Best for

Architecture and design portfolios, product launches, minimal pitch decks, engineering docs.
