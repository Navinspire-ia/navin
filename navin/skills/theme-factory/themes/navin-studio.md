# Navin Studio

Mood: Ink on paper with one lime accent; calm, exact, unhurried. The house theme and the default for anything without a stated style.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#f4f3ee` - page and slide ground (Paper)
- **Surface**: `#faf9f6` - cards, inputs, callouts (Cream)
- **Text**: `#17171a` - headings and body (Ink)
- **Accent**: `#c6e88a` - the single highlight, buttons, key figure (Lime)
- **Secondary**: `#0e7490` - links, second data series (Teal)

## Typography

- **Heading**: Inter
- **Body**: IBM Plex Sans

## Mode

Light by default. Dark variant: swap Background to `#17171a`, Surface to `#121214`, Text to `#f4f3ee`; Accent stays Lime; Secondary becomes `#0ea5e9`.

## Best for

Product decks, reports, dashboards, documentation, anything that represents Navin.
