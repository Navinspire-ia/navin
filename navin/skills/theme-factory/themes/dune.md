# Dune

Mood: Late light on sand; amber warmth, long shadows, a desert stillness.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#fbf6ec` - sunlit sand ground
- **Surface**: `#f3e8d2` - cards and callouts
- **Text**: `#2a2217` - dark earth for type
- **Accent**: `#febc2e` - emphasis, highlights, key figures (Amber)
- **Secondary**: `#b8743a` - rust for rules and a second series

## Typography

- **Heading**: DM Serif Display
- **Body**: Inter

## Mode

Light by default. Dark variant: Background `#2a2217`, Surface `#3a2f20`, Text `#fbf6ec`; Accent stays Amber, Secondary becomes `#e0a66a`.

## Best for

Travel and hospitality, editorial features, end-of-year reviews, lifestyle brands.
