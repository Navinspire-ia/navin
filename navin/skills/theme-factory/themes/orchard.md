# Orchard

Mood: Fresh, bright and friendly; leaf and lime on cream with a sky-blue breeze.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#faf9f6` - cream ground (Cream)
- **Surface**: `#eef5e4` - pale leaf cards
- **Text**: `#17171a` - headings and body (Ink)
- **Accent**: `#6fbf6a` - buttons and emphasis (Leaf)
- **Secondary**: `#0ea5e9` - links and a second series (Sky)

## Typography

- **Heading**: Outfit
- **Body**: Inter

## Mode

Light by default. Dark variant: Background `#17171a`, Surface `#1e2a1d`, Text `#f4f3ee`; Accent becomes Lime `#c6e88a`, Secondary stays Sky.

## Best for

Community programmes, health and nutrition, schools, onboarding guides, newsletters.
