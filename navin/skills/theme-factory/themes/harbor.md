# Harbor

Mood: Cold water and steel; trustworthy, measured, maritime without the kitsch.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#f4f3ee` - page ground (Paper)
- **Surface**: `#e3eef2` - cards and callouts, a pale sea-mist
- **Text**: `#12303a` - deep harbor blue for headings and body
- **Accent**: `#0e7490` - emphasis, rules, key figures (Teal)
- **Secondary**: `#0ea5e9` - links and a second data series (Sky)

## Typography

- **Heading**: Source Serif 4
- **Body**: Inter

## Mode

Light by default. Dark variant: Background `#12303a`, Surface `#0d242c`, Text `#f4f3ee`; Accent becomes Sky, Secondary becomes `#8fd0e8`.

## Best for

Financial reports, consulting decks, compliance documents, board updates.
