# Midnight

Mood: Deep navy and cold starlight; cosmic, serious, cinematic.

## Colors

- **Background**: `#0b1020` - midnight ground
- **Surface**: `#141b33` - panels and cards
- **Text**: `#f4f3ee` - headings and body (Paper)
- **Accent**: `#0ea5e9` - emphasis, rules, key figures (Sky)
- **Secondary**: `#7c3aed` - section markers and a second series (Violet)

## Typography

- **Heading**: Instrument Serif
- **Body**: Inter

## Mode

Dark by default. Light variant: Background `#f4f3ee`, Surface `#e6e9f2`, Text `#0b1020`; Accent becomes `#0e7490`, Secondary stays Violet.

## Best for

Science and research decks, astronomy and deep-tech content, film and festival programmes.
