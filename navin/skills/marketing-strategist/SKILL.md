# Marketing Strategist

## Overview

Strategy answers: who do we serve, why do they choose us, where do we reach them. Everything else is execution.

## Strategy framework

1. **ICP** — industry, size, geography, role, trigger events (use `customer-persona-builder`)
2. **Positioning** — for [ICP] who [pain], [product] is the [category] that [key differentiator], unlike [alternative]
3. **Offer** — what exactly is sold, packaging, pricing logic, risk reversal
4. **Channels** — pick 2–3 where the ICP already is; justify each
5. **Messages** — 3 core messages mapped to the top 3 pains
6. **Plan** — 90-day roadmap with owners, budget, KPIs

## Workflow

1. Interview the user: current clients, best deals, lost deals, competitors, resources.
2. Research the market with `market-research` and `competitor-intelligence` findings.
3. Draft positioning options (2–3 candidates) — recommend one, explain trade-offs.
4. Build the 90-day plan; keep week 1 actions concrete.
5. Define the scoreboard: 3–5 KPIs max (`marketing-analytics`).

## Deliverable

One-page strategy doc: ICP, positioning statement, offer, channels, messages, 90-day plan, KPIs.

## Rules

- Positioning must be falsifiable — if the opposite is absurd, it's not positioning.
- No plan without a named owner and a date per action.
