# Channel logos

Bundled with the web UI, so the Channels page works offline and never calls a
third party to draw a logo.

- facebook.svg, instagram.svg, linkedin.svg, matrix.svg, mattermost.svg,
  reddit.svg, signal.svg, telegram.svg, whatsapp.svg: Simple Icons
  (https://simpleicons.org), released under CC0-1.0. Each logo stays a
  trademark of its owner and only names the service a channel connects to.
- email.svg: the "mail" glyph of Lucide (https://lucide.dev), ISC License,
  Copyright (c) Lucide Contributors 2022, portions Copyright (c) Cole Bemis
  2013-2022 (Feather, MIT License).

Discord, Microsoft Teams, Slack and TikTok reuse the app connector icons of
../connectors (see navin/connectors/NOTICE.md).
